# Licence and rights

Part of [MBDef]({{repo_url}}).

- **Our annotations are licensed {{licence_name}}.** That covers the selection, term merging, relevance scores and decisions.
- **The definition texts are quotations.** Their copyright stays with the papers' authors and publishers.
- **What OpenAlex records for each paper** (`papers.open_licence`):
  - {{redis_papers}} papers ({{redis_defs}} definitions) have a CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0 / public-domain version;
  - {{nc_papers}} papers ({{nc_defs}} definitions) have a non-commercial CC version;
  - {{nolic_papers}} papers have no recorded licence.
- If you are a rights holder and want a quotation removed, open a discussion on this repository.
